Share numbers

How share and minimum difficulties are shown on Status and Clients. The Gateway counts in hashes, the same scale as DATUM and mempool. A miner counts in units of 232 hashes, so its own number is smaller. Only the display changes, so you can switch any time.

Auto refresh

How often Status, Clients, Threads and Coinbaser update themselves. Pages stop updating while their tab is in the background.

Gateway

maintenance

Restarts the DATUM Gateway process (not your Bitcoin node or Umbrel). Miners disconnect for a few seconds and reconnect on their own. Useful after a node restart or if the Gateway looks stuck.
